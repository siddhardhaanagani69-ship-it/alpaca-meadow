# Crazy Alpaca (WePlay) — Complete Game Description

> **Also known as:** Alpaca Challenge, Alpaca Game, Alpaquita, Sheep Crush (in some regions/communities)
> **Platform:** Mini-game inside the **WePlay** social party-game app (iOS / Android)
> **Genre:** Layered 3-tile matching puzzle with co-op team play

---

## 1. Overview

Crazy Alpaca is a daily puzzle challenge in which you tap tiles (called "cards") from a stacked, overlapping pile and send them into a small holding slot at the bottom of the screen. When three identical cards land in the slot, they combine and disappear. If the slot fills up with cards that don't form a set of three, you lose.

It is widely recognized as being modeled on the Chinese viral hit **Sheep a Sheep (羊了个羊)** — same core mechanic (layered tiles, match three, limited slot, brutal second level), same daily-ranking structure — but reskinned with a farm/alpaca theme and expanded with a **3-player cooperative mode**, team trading, and a summonable alpaca power-up.

Its reputation among players is that it's a real test of patience: the first level is trivially easy, and the second level is notoriously hard.

## 2. Visual & Graphic Style

### Overall art direction
- **Cute, cartoony, casual-mobile aesthetic** consistent with the rest of WePlay: soft rounded shapes, bold outlines, saturated friendly colors, and a light "farm/pasture" theme.
- The mascot is a **chibi-style alpaca** — big head, fluffy wool, expressive face — which appears on the main screen, as the summonable helper, and as a collectible, dressable skin.

### The cards (tiles)
- Square/rounded-square tiles, each showing a single simple icon on a light background, so they read instantly on a small phone screen.
- The icons are everyday farm, kitchen, and clothing objects (full list in §6): carrots, cotton/wool, cheese, skewers, knife-and-fork, peas, sprouts, gloves, hats, yarn, socks, and a **purple jewel/diamond**.
- The jewel is the only card with a distinctive purple color, which makes it stand out visually because it has a special function (charging the alpaca gauge).

### The board
- Cards are piled in **many overlapping layers**. Cards partially covered by another card are shown **dimmed/greyed out** and can't be tapped; only fully exposed cards are bright and active. This light-vs-dark contrast is the main visual language that tells you what's playable.
- The layout is a symmetrical, pattern-like arrangement in the center of the screen, plus (typically in this genre) side stacks where only the top card is visible. The **map changes every day**.
- Hidden cards are a key part of the design: you often clear what looks like the last card only to reveal another one underneath.

### The slot / tray
- A horizontal tray at the bottom holds your picked cards. Identical cards group next to each other so you can see how close you are to a triple.
- A **purple gauge** sits below the slot and fills as you collect jewel cards.
- When the tray is nearly full, the game enters an **SOS state** (a danger warning).

### Seasonal and cosmetic visuals
- **Map backgrounds/skins** can be changed. For example, Season 1 offered a limited "Light of Spring" background with a cherry-blossom feel.
- **Alpaca skins** (40+) let you dress the alpaca: colored coats (dark, green, gold, orange, pink, purple, blue, mint, lavender, red, yellow, spotted), and costumes like Santa/Christmas, Angel with halo, Chef, Firefighter, Worker with hard hat, Scholar, Ninja, Nurse, Doctor, Phantom Thief, Jiangshi (hopping-vampire hat), Diving, Rich (yellow suit), Music (with animated notes), and more.

## 3. Animations & Game Feel

> This section describes the animation style players experience in this genre and in WePlay's polished casual games. Exact timings and effects are **not officially documented**.

- **Tap-to-fly:** When you tap an exposed card, it slides/flies smoothly from the board down into its spot in the tray, usually with an easing motion (quick start, soft landing).
- **Auto-sorting:** Cards in the tray shuffle over so identical icons sit together, giving you instant visual feedback.
- **Triple clear:** When three matching cards meet, they pop/merge and vanish with a small burst effect, and the remaining cards slide left to close the gap.
- **Reveal:** When a covering card is removed, the card underneath brightens from dim to full color, signalling that it's now playable.
- **Shuffle:** The Mix item animates the cards rearranging themselves across the board.
- **Alpaca summon:** When the purple gauge is full, the alpaca appears as a special event and wipes cards from the tray and a chosen top-layer card.
- **Animated skins:** Some skins have their own motion, such as the Music Alpaca, whose musical notes move.
- **Win/lose screens:** A congratulatory message plays when you clear the hard level; failure brings up the retry/share prompt.

## 4. How the Game Works (Core Rules)

1. **Tap** any fully exposed card on the board. It moves into the bottom slot.
2. **Match three** identical cards in the slot to clear them.
3. **Don't fill the slot.** If the slot fills with cards that can't make triples, you're in SOS / out of room and the attempt fails.
4. **Clear every card** on the board to win.

### Two stages
| Stage | What it's like |
|---|---|
| **Easy** | Only three card types appear (carrots, cotton, cheese). It's so easy you effectively can't fail — even tapping randomly works, and you can't really reach SOS. Clearing it grants two random items. |
| **Hard** | Many more card types, many more layers, hidden cards everywhere. This is where the real challenge is. In 3-player mode, this is where teamwork kicks in. |

**Tip:** If you complete the carrot/cotton/cheese quests during the Easy stage, you start Hard with two or three items instead of one.

### Winning and recording
- Clearing the Hard stage returns you to the main alpaca screen with a congratulations message, and your alpaca is added to your **team's ranking**.
- Your profile shows your **fastest clear time for the day**. If you don't clear that day, it shows "Game Failed." Records reset daily, but your best time is kept in your stats.

## 5. Game Modes

### Solo (Individual)
- You must clear the puzzle yourself.
- After a failure, you're offered a chance to continue if you **share** the game. If you fail again, continuing costs **10 coins**. Fail after that and you must start over — so effectively you get two extra chances.

### 3-Player Match (Co-op)
- You're matched with two teammates. It's a voice-enabled game, but most players mute and use text chat since they're concentrating.
- If **any one** teammate clears the puzzle, **all three** get credit for the clear.
- Teammates can **request and pass cards** to one another, and there is a **shared/public warehouse (storage)** for moving cards between players.
- If a teammate leaves mid-game, clearing can become near impossible.

### Teams & rankings
- Players are grouped into teams by **MBTI personality type**. Once chosen, your team can't be changed.
- Successful alpacas are counted in team rankings; the list updates after one day.
- Leaving games repeatedly restricts matchmaking for 2 minutes, then 5 minutes for repeat offenders.
- Crazy Alpaca is the only "battle mini-game" in WePlay that doesn't charge coins to enter.

## 7. Items / Props (Power-ups)

Items are mainly earned by completing **quests/orders** during the game. Getting items early makes the Hard stage far more manageable.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Remove** | Takes three cards out of the slot and sets them aside face-down, freeing space. |
| **Go Back (Undo)** | Returns the last card you picked to its original spot on the board. Handy for escaping an SOS or a bad tap. |
| **Free Choice** | Lets you pick a card that's partly covered (as long as you can see it). Extremely powerful. |
| **Clear (Hammer)** | Deletes a card from the top layer. Be careful — what's underneath may not help you. |
| **Mix (Shuffle)** | Shuffles the cards on the board. |

## 8. The Alpaca Power (Special Ability)

- Matching **purple jewel** cards fills the purple gauge under the slot.
- When the gauge is full, you can **call the Alpaca**. You choose a top-layer card to erase, and **all cards in your slot are cleared** at the same time.
- If you don't make a selection, only one card type disappears at random.
- It can backfire: if you or a teammate were collecting specific cards for a quest, a surprise alpaca can wipe them.

### Pro strategy for the alpaca (3-player)
- **Announce it first.** Always tell your team before calling the alpaca.
- **Stage an SOS.** Before the alpaca is called, the two other players deliberately fill their slots into SOS. The alpaca wipes those slots, turning a crisis into a reset.
- **Protect jewels.** Community tips stress never discarding diamonds by mistake and never leaving them buried.
- **Split roles.** One player goes ahead as the "runner," while the two support players keep the warehouse open to pass cards. Each player clears something different, and items are saved for the end.

## 9. Quests, Orders & Seasons

### Quests / Orders
- Collecting the requested cards completes an order and rewards items. Early in the game orders ask for a lot, so community advice is not to obsess over them at first.

### Season 1 challenge
Season 1 ran for 60 days with limited rewards (a new item and the "Light of Spring" background). Goals included collecting large amounts of resources (carrots, corn, milk, wool, peas, cheese), completing hundreds of orders, clearing a set number of games (including some within 15 minutes), using tools, and triggering Fever Time.

## 11. Tips for Winning

1. **Farm items early.** Focus on quests at the start of Hard; items are your lifeline later.
2. **Never tap blindly.** Before picking, check what's underneath and how many of that icon are exposed.
3. **Keep slot space.** Avoid holding many different single cards; aim for triples.
4. **Aim for the middle** when you don't already hold matching cards — central stacks usually hide more.
5. **Save items for the end** where hidden cards cause the most trouble.
6. **Communicate** (chat or voice) in 3-player mode, especially before calling the alpaca.
7. **Use the shared warehouse** to pass cards to whoever is closest to finishing.
